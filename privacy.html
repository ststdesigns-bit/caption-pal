Caption Pal Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

The short version

Caption Pal does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Your conversation history stays on your iPhone, and you can delete it at any time.

Who we are

Caption Pal is an iPhone app made by Jim Elliott, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the developer of Caption Pal.

Microphone and speech recognition

Caption Pal shows live captions of what people say. To do that, it asks for permission to use your iPhone's microphone and Apple's Speech Recognition. The app only listens while you have captions turned on, and it does not record or save audio files.

Speech is turned into text by Apple's built-in speech recognition. When possible this happens on your device. If on-device recognition is not available for your language or device, Apple may process the audio on its servers to produce the text. That processing is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we never receive or see that audio or text.

You can turn off microphone or speech recognition access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security on your iPhone. Captions will not work without them.

Translation

One-way and two-way translation use Apple's Translation framework, which translates on your device. The first time you use a language, your iPhone may ask to download that language from Apple. That download is handled by Apple, and your conversations are not sent to us.

Conversation history

Captions and translations you see in the app may be saved only on your iPhone so you can scroll back through them. They are not uploaded to us or to any server we run. You can clear the history in the app at any time, and deleting the app removes everything it stored.

If you use iPhone or iCloud backups, Apple may include the app's local data in your backup, as it does for other apps. That is controlled by your own Apple settings.

What we collect and share

  • We collect no personal data.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share any data with anyone.

If you choose to email us, we will use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Children

Caption Pal is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, there is nothing for us to share or sell.

Not a medical device

Caption Pal is a helpful communication tool, not a medical device or hearing aid. Captions and translations may contain mistakes, so please don't rely on them for medical, legal, or emergency decisions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
