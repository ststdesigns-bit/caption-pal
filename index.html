What Caption Pal does

Caption Pal turns what people say into large, easy-to-read text on your iPhone, as they speak. It started as a way to help a family member who is hard of hearing follow conversations again, and it can also translate between languages so two people can talk without sharing one.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your conversations stay on your phone.

How to use the three modes

1. Captions

  1. Tap Start Listening and hold the phone near the person speaking.
  2. The newest line is bright and bold; older lines turn soft gray and scroll up, so you can look back.
  3. Tap Next to start a new line (for example, when a different person starts talking).
  4. Tap Stop Listening when you're done. Clear erases the conversation.

2. One-way translation

  1. Open Settings > Translation and choose One-way.
  2. Pick the language they speak and the language you want to read.
  3. Tap Start Listening. The translation is shown large, with the original in smaller text underneath.

3. Two-way translation

  1. Choose Two-way in Settings > Translation and pick the two languages.
  2. Lay the phone flat between you. The screen splits in two, one half for each person, each in their own language.
  3. Each person taps their language button before speaking. Their words appear translated on the other person's side.
  4. Prefer sitting side by side or passing the phone? Choose the Same direction layout instead of Facing each other.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Caption Pal ask for the microphone and Speech Recognition?

It needs the microphone to hear the conversation and Apple's Speech Recognition to turn speech into text. If you said "Don't Allow" earlier, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone and Speech Recognition and turn Caption Pal on.

It asked me to download a language. Is that normal?

Yes. Translation happens on your iPhone using Apple's Translation feature, so the first time you use a language your iPhone downloads it from Apple. Wi-Fi is recommended. You can manage downloaded languages in Settings > Apps > Translate > Downloaded Languages (location may vary by iOS version).

Which iPhones and iOS versions are supported?

Caption Pal runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

The captions are wrong or slow. What can I do?

Hold the phone closer to the speaker, reduce background noise, and make sure the right language is selected. Short pauses between sentences help. You can also adjust the pause length in Settings.

Does it work without internet?

Translation works offline once languages are downloaded. Speech recognition works on-device for many languages; for others, Apple may need an internet connection.

Is my conversation saved or shared?

Conversation history is stored only on your iPhone, and you can clear it any time. Nothing is sent to us. See the Privacy Policy.

Please note

Caption Pal is a communication aid, not a medical device or hearing aid. Captions and translations may not always be accurate. Don't rely on them for medical, legal, safety, or emergency decisions.
